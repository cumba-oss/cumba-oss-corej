# ADaMIG 1.3 Non-Extensible Controlled Terminology

Variables with non-extensible (closed) codelists from ADaMIG v1.3.
These codelists do not allow sponsor-defined values.

**Total:** 7 non-extensible codelists covering 30 variable-codelist pairs

> **Note:** Indexed variables (APxxSDTF, PHwEDTF, TRxxSDTF, etc.) are excluded
